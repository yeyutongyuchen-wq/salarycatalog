California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Registered Nurses was $140,270, compared with $97,550 nationally. The median hourly wage was $67.44 in California and $46.90 in the United States. OEWS employment was 338,940 in California and 3,379,720 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 43.8 percent higher than the national median.

Data source and method

Figures are from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) May 2025 release. A wage shown as $239,200 or More means the estimate falls in BLS's highest, open-ended wage interval and is a lower bound, not a maximum. In tables, — means the estimate is missing or suppressed by BLS; # means suppressed because of insufficient reporting units; * means the estimate is not comparable.
