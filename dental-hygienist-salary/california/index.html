California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Dental Hygienists was $124,930, compared with $98,100 nationally. The median hourly wage was $60.06 in California and $47.16 in the United States. OEWS employment was 24,500 in California and 222,740 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 27.3 percent higher than the national median.
