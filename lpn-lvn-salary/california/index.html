California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Licensed Practical and Licensed Vocational Nurses was $79,750, compared with $64,400 nationally. The median hourly wage was $38.34 in California and $30.96 in the United States. OEWS employment was 82,850 in California and 648,410 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 23.8 percent higher than the national median.

Data source and method
