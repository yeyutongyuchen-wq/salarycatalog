California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Medical Assistants was $49,660, compared with $45,690 nationally. The median hourly wage was $23.88 in California and $21.97 in the United States. OEWS employment was 117,060 in California and 817,870 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 8.7 percent higher than the national median.

Data source and method

Figures are from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) May 2025 release. A wage shown as $239,200 or More means the estimate falls in BLS's highest, open-ended wage interval and is a lower bound, not a maximum.
