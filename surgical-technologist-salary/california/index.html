California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Surgical Technologists was $81,310, compared with $64,650 nationally. The median hourly wage was $39.09 in California and $31.08 in the United States. OEWS employment was 11,920 in California and 117,460 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 25.8 percent higher than the national median.
