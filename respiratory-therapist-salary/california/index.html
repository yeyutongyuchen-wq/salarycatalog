California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Respiratory Therapists was $104,820, compared with $82,280 nationally. The median hourly wage was $50.40 in California and $39.56 in the United States. OEWS employment was 18,650 in California and 139,790 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 27.4 percent higher than the national median.
