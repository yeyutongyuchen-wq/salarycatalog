California vs. the national average

In California, the OEWS May 2025 median annual wage for Radiologic Technologists and Technicians was $121,260, compared with $80,110 nationally. The median hourly wage was $58.30 in California and $38.52 in the United States. OEWS employment was 20,900 in California and 230,490 nationwide for this occupation. The California median was 51.4 percent higher than the national median.
